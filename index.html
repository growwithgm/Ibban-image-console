Pick the category, copy the prompt, every colour covered.

You never write a prompt by hand. The category sets the rules, and the master prompts make the AI write the final image prompts after it has looked at the real product photographs.

1The run

Three passes, in this order. Pass 3 comes last so the shared images match a batch you have already approved.

  1. pass 1 — prompt writing Pick the category, paste Prompt A

    Choose it in section 2, attach front and back for every colourway, paste Prompt A. It returns one prompt per colour plus a HERO COLOUR block. No images yet.

  2. pass 2 — per SKU One new chat per colour

    Attach that colour's front and back, paste that colour's block. Five images for that variant.

  3. pass 3 — per product Run Prompt B once

    Hero colour's photographs plus its HERO COLOUR block. Three shared images used across every variant.

Image count: (5 × colourways) + 3. A product in 5 colours = 28 images.

2The product

Press the store category. It sets the product type, how it is worn and the shot rules inside both prompts, and it overrides what the AI reads from the photos.

Holiday, New In, Sale and Girls hold mixed products, so they have no button — press the product's own type instead.

5Shot map

      
Five shots follow the colour. Three are made once and shared.

      

        

          
#ShotScopeWhy it exists

          
        

      

      
The two text images are shared on purpose: generating them per colour risks several different sets of numbers. The lifestyle shot is shared to hold the count down, and it is the first one to move back to per SKU if the hero colour in the gallery ever starts causing confusion.

7QA before upload

      
Check every batch against the reference photograph. Drift concentrates in the macros and in movement, where less of the product is in frame.

      

        

          
CheckWhat fails in practice

          
            
Product type unchangedA dress or tunic becomes an open coat, a bag gains straps or pockets, swimwear changes coverage

            
Embroidery matchesMotifs invented, mirrored, or scaled up to fill a macro frame

            
Thread and ground colour holdColour shifts a step warmer or cooler between shots

            
Styling base identicalA piece changes colour or cut in a crop, or trousers appear under a piece worn on its own

            
Same model across the batchFace changes between shots in the same run

            
Model reads under 30Styling or lighting ages her

            
Scale looks rightA bag, wallet or piece of jewellery grows or shrinks against the body

            
Safe square respectedHead, hem or product edge sits outside the central square and a 1:1 crop cuts it

            
No stray textInvented labels, tags or signage on shots 1 to 5 and the lifestyle shot

9Uploading to Shopify

      
Gallery order: the selected colour's five first, the shared three after.

      
    
      
      
        
    • Assign shot 1 to its variant in Media, not just upload it to the gallery. Without the assignment the swatch does not change the image, and the feed falls back to the product's first image, so a Cream shopper sees a Black ad.
      • 
        
    • The three shared images are assigned to no variant and stay at the end of the gallery.
      • 
        
    • Alt text: product name plus colour. File name readable, for example nuria-dress-black-front.webp.
      • 
        
    • Export as WebP or JPEG, roughly 200–300 KB each.
      • 
        
    • After changing a main image, the Meta catalogue keeps serving the old image_link until the next feed sync.
